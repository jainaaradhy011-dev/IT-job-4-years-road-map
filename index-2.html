Your four-year roadmap

What top recruiters check

Problem solving

Around 300 well-chosen problems, with the patterns you can explain out loud.

Real projects

Three deployed projects with a README, a live link and clear commits.

Experience

Two internships or open-source contributions on your resume.

Communication

Mock interviews, a clean resume and a profile that shows your work.

What each package level needs

Solid start

4-8 LPA

Large IT services companies, mostly through campus drives.

  • Strong basics in one language
  • Aptitude and reasoning practice
  • CS fundamentals and 1 project

Strong offer

10-20 LPA

Product startups and mid-size tech companies.

  • 250+ problems solved
  • 2 deployed projects
  • One internship

Top tier

25+ LPA

Top product companies and high-growth firms.

  • 400+ problems, with hard ones
  • System design and strong internship
  • Open-source or standout project

Ranges are indicative and vary by company, city and hiring year. Check current offers on company career pages and placement reports.

Five mistakes that cost offers

Tutorial hopping

Finish one course, then build something without a guide.

Starting late

Internship and placement doors open a year before you think.

Only solving, never explaining

Interviews score how you talk through a solution.

Empty GitHub

Recruiters check it. Commit weekly and write READMEs.

Ignoring CS subjects

OS, DBMS and networks show up in almost every interview.
